One question at a time

Put the deck on your home screen

Takes about twenty seconds. Once it's there it opens like a normal app, full screen, no address bar, and it remembers the questions you starred.

1

Open the link in Safari

If you tapped the link from a text or an email, you might be in a mini browser inside that app. Look at the bottom of the screen for the compass icon and tap it to jump over to real Safari.

Already in Safari? Skip straight to step 2.

2

Tap the share button

It's the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bar at the bottom of the screen. On an older iPhone it may sit at the top right instead.

3

Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen

A menu slides up. Scroll past the row of apps and the copy and bookmark options. Add to Home Screen is in the lower list, next to a small plus in a square.

Don't see it? Tap Edit Actions at the very bottom of that list and switch it on. iPhone hides it sometimes.

4

Tap Add

Top right corner. You can rename it first if you want, it comes in as Questions. The icon lands on your home screen on the last page.

That's it. Open it from the home screen and take turns.

Whoever's phone it is keeps their own starred questions. Passing on a question only removes it from that phone, so you each get your own deck.
